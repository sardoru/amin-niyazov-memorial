# Family Memorial Text — Аминджан Ирматович Ниязов

## Key New Facts from Family Text (not in Wikipedia)

### Personal Details
- Full first name: **Аминджан** (Aminjan), not just Amin
- Born in kishlak **Ak-Tepe near Margilan** (not just "Fergana Oblast")
- Father **Irmat-aka** was a sharecropper (izdolshchik) for wealthy landowners
- Started education in old-style school, then modern school
- Excelled at **physics and mathematics** from childhood
- During Civil War famine, ate grass and oil cake (жмых)
- Helped distribute expropriated grain to starving people as youth

### Education & Early Career
- Was offered by **Akmal Ikramov** (1st Secretary of CP Uzbekistan 1929-37) to study in Germany with 70 other youth — **declined** because he couldn't leave his elderly mother and young family
- At Moscow Industrial Academy, was **classmate of Nikita Khrushchev**
- Studied only on excellent marks, excelled at "machine parts", "strength of materials", "theoretical mechanics"
- Lived in a one-room apartment with his family in Moscow

### Chirchik
- After graduating 1934, **volunteered** to go as an ordinary worker to the construction site
- Rose from **foreman → site manager → chief engineer → head of construction control committee** of the Council of People's Commissars

### Fergana Textile Industry
- Some specialists tried to prove Fergana was climatically unsuitable for textile/silk-reeling factories
- Niyazov **proved them wrong**, became one of the construction leaders
- Sent ~900 young workers to Russian textile centers for training; they returned to staff new factories

### Land-Water Reform (1920s)
- Personally implemented land-water reform — taking land and irrigation channels from bais (rich landowners) and giving to dehkans
- Some peasants under religious influence refused land: "I'll die of hunger but won't take the bai's land"
- Awarded **Honorary Diploma of the Government of Uzbekistan** for land reform achievements

### WWII Details
- **Chkalov Aviation Factory** was operational within **42 days** of evacuation to Uzbekistan
- **280 new industrial enterprises** were built and operational during the war
- New industries appeared: aviation, machine tools, heavy industry, non-ferrous and ferrous metallurgy
- His role in timely financing was crucial to all of this

### As First Secretary (1950-1955) — Major Achievements
- In 1950, Uzbekistan collected **3,000,000 tons of raw cotton** for the first time in history
- **Mechanization:** 22,428 cotton-harvesting machines SHM-48 (made at Tashselmash, Tashkent) deployed 1950-1955
- **Education:** Built 42 schools, provided full textbooks/supplies, organized state meals for 1,000 boarding school children
- **Medical institutions created:**
  - Medical Institute in Andijan (1955)
  - Institute of Regional Medicine of Academy of Sciences (later Institute of Endocrinology)
  - Research Institute of Oncology and Radiology
- **Industrial construction:** Auto-repair factory in Andijan, diesel power station in Bukhara, Namangan HPP #3, TPP in Karshi, Angren GRES
- **Agricultural support:** Established "Suburban Zone" around cities for vegetable/dairy supply, bought 3,000 pedigree heifers from abroad
- **USSR Government decrees implemented:** Higher procurement prices for cotton/karakul/silk, development of Central Fergana, Kairakum reservoir (with Tajik SSR), new irrigation systems

### Foreign Policy
- Worked with **Molotov (USSR Foreign Minister)** to establish contacts with leaders of Bandung Conference (29 non-aligned nations)
- Organized first visits to USSR (including Uzbekistan) of:
  - **Jawaharlal Nehru and Indira Gandhi** (1955)
  - **U Nu** (Prime Minister of Burma)
  - Official Soviet delegation (Bulganin, Khrushchev) to India, Burma, Afghanistan

### Dismissal
- Did NOT join any political factions/groupings
- Did NOT support Khrushchev's intrigues — this was the "subconscious motive" for his removal
- December 22, 1955: Extraordinary Plenum of CC, Moscow representatives arrived
- Same day appointed Minister of Municipal Economy
- **Worked 17 years without a vacation** — this destroyed his health

### Post-Dismissal
- As Minister of Municipal Economy: pioneered **natural gas supply** to Uzbekistan residents (first time ever)
- In retirement: helped build **Tashkent-Kokand highway**
- Named associates: Akram Khodjaev, Minovar Tursunov, Abdusamat Bobokhodjaev, Narmukammadi Khudayberdyev, General-Lieutenant Burkhan Sirazhdinov, Zaynutdin Abdurahimov

### Family
- **Wife:** Salimakhon Niyazova — "keeper of the family hearth, faithful helper and reliable support"
- **Children:**
  - **Adkhamjon Niyazov** (1930-1999) — Associate Professor at Polytechnic Institute
  - **Khalida Niyazova** (1933-1996) — Doctor/physician
  - **Rustam Niyazov** (b. 1938) — Candidate of geological-mineralogical sciences, General Director of "Uzbekhydrogeology" Research Institute
  - **Lola Niyazova** (b. 1940) — Teacher of Russian language and literature

### Final Honor
- November 1973: Turned 70, awarded title **"Honored Builder of the Uzbek SSR"** (Заслуженный строитель Узбекской ССР)
- December 1973: Passed away
